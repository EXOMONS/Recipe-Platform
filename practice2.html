Doro Wat Recipe

Doro Wat is a traditional Ethiopian chicken stew prepared with berbere spice and served with injera.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Onions
  • Berbere spice
  • Butter
  • Eggs

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients.
  2. Cook onions slowly.
  3. Add spices and chicken.
  4. Cook until ready.
  5. Serve with injera.
