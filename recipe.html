Doro Wat

Doro Wat is one of the most popular Ethiopian dishes. It is prepared with chicken, onions, berbere spice, butter, and boiled eggs.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Onions
  • Berbere Spice
  • Butter
  • Boiled Eggs
  • Salt

Cooking Steps

  1. Cut and clean the chicken.
  2. Cook chopped onions until soft.
  3. Add butter and berbere spice.
  4. Add the chicken and cook well.
  5. Add boiled eggs.
  6. Serve hot with injera.
